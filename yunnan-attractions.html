This guide follows the natural rhythm of a ten-day Yunnan trip: settle into Kunming’s easy city life, climb into Lijiang’s Naxi heartland, then finish beside Erhai beneath the Cangshan range. Descriptions are in English; Chinese names are retained for easy searching and navigation.

Kunming 昆明

Days 1–3 · Spring City
01 · CITY PARK

翠湖公园 GREEN LAKE PARK

A willow-fringed lake and pavilion garden in central Kunming. In winter, migratory black-headed gulls turn the water and promenades into one of the city’s liveliest scenes. Come in the morning for local dancers, musicians and an easy first walk after arrival.

1–1.5 hoursBest in the morningExcellent in December

02 · OLD QUARTER

昆明老街 KUNMING OLD STREET

Historic shopfronts, restored courtyards and snack stalls around Guanghua Street and Qianwang Street. It is commercial, but still the best central district for seeing fragments of old Kunming and sampling local food after dark.

1.5–2 hoursLate afternoon

03 · CITY LIFE

南屏街 NANPING STREET

Kunming’s central pedestrian shopping strip: bright, busy and contemporary. Its value is less about monuments and more about dinner, people-watching and the contrast between modern Kunming and the neighbouring old streets.

1–2 hoursEvening

04 · UNESCO KARST

石林 STONE FOREST

A labyrinth of blade-like limestone pillars formed over millions of years and protected as part of the South China Karst UNESCO site. The Great Stone Forest is dramatic and dense; the Small Stone Forest opens into broader, more photogenic views linked to Sani folklore.

Lijiang 丽江

Days 4–7 · Naxi highlands
05 · UNESCO OLD TOWN

丽江古城 LIJIANG OLD TOWN

An 800-year-old web of stone lanes, canals and Naxi timber houses that once served the Tea Horse Road. The busiest lanes are highly commercial; the reward comes early in the morning, along the quieter waterways, and from the roofline viewpoints at sunset.

Half day + eveningGo before 9 amEssential
PHOTO SOURCE ↗
06 · NAXI HISTORY

木府 MUFU PALACE

The reconstructed residence of the Mu clan, hereditary Naxi rulers under the Ming and Qing empires. Ceremonial halls, carved timber details and climbing courtyards give the old town historical context; the upper terraces offer fine roof views.

1.5–2 hoursInside the old town

07 · 3,650 M

玉龙雪山牦牛坪 YAK MEADOW

A broad alpine meadow reached by cableway, with forest edges, grazing yaks and a sweeping face-on view of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain. It is quieter and more spacious than Glacier Park, trading extreme altitude for a slower mountain landscape.

2–3 hoursCold and windyWeather dependent

08 · 4,506 M

冰川公园 GLACIER PARK

The mountain’s headline experience: a cable car rises into a stark world of snow, rock and glacier views. A boardwalk continues above the station. The scenery is dramatic, but the altitude is serious—move slowly and descend if symptoms become significant.

Reserve aheadSub-zero possibleHigh altitude

09 · GLACIAL WATER

蓝月谷 BLUE MOON VALLEY

Mineral-rich glacial water forms luminous turquoise pools, cascades and pale terraces below the snow mountain. Some sections are landscaped, yet clear winter light makes the colour striking. It combines naturally with either Glacier Park or Yak Meadow.

1.5–2.5 hoursEasy walkingBest in sunshine

10 · OPEN-AIR PERFORMANCE

《印象丽江》 IMPRESSION LIJIANG

Zhang Yimou’s outdoor production uses hundreds of performers, vivid ethnic dress, drums and song against the real mountain backdrop. It is spectacle rather than documentary, but the scale and high-altitude setting make it memorable on a clear day.

About 1 hourOutdoor seatingBring a warm layer

11 · EARLY NAXI CENTRE

白沙古镇 BAISHA OLD TOWN

An early centre of Naxi power beneath the mountain, quieter and rougher-edged than central Lijiang. Seek out the historic Baisha murals, small workshops and lanes where the snow peak appears beyond low traditional roofs.

2–3 hoursBest in the morning
PHOTO SETTING ↗
Mountain-day choice

Yak Meadow and Glacier Park use different cableways and create very different days. Choose Glacier Park for the high-altitude snow experience; choose Yak Meadow for a gentler, quieter landscape. Trying to combine both usually makes the day rushed.

Dali 大理

Days 7–10 · Cangshan & Erhai
12 · WALLED TOWN

大理古城 DALI OLD TOWN

A spacious historic grid set between Cangshan and Erhai, with gates, Bai-style buildings and a lively evening scene. It is commercial but easy to explore, and works especially well as a base: wander Renmin Road, climb a gate and let dinner become the itinerary.

Half day + eveningGood baseMountain views

13 · BAI HERITAGE

喜洲古镇 XIZHOU OLD TOWN

A prosperous former trading town celebrated for ornate Bai courtyard houses, village lanes and fields under Cangshan. Look beyond the busy square, visit a historic courtyard and try xizhou baba, the local sweet or savoury baked flatbread.

2–3 hoursMorning visitTry 喜洲粑粑
PHOTO SOURCE ↗
14 · HIGH-PLATEAU LAKE

洱海 ERHAI LAKE

A 40-kilometre freshwater lake holding Dali between blue water and the Cangshan wall. Rather than racing around the full circuit, follow a western-shore section by bicycle or car through villages, wetlands and open lake views.

Half or full dayWest shore for cyclingWindy in winter

15 · 9TH-CENTURY LANDMARK

崇圣寺三塔 THREE PAGODAS

Dali’s defining monument: a tall Tang-era central pagoda flanked by two later towers, with Cangshan rising behind. Start at the reflection pond for the classic view, then decide how far to climb through the large reconstructed temple complex.

2–3 hoursSome uphill walking
