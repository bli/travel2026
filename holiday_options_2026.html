Seven ways to spend summer—or escape it.

Comparable 12-day routes for mid-December 2026 to early January 2027, designed for a comfortable pace from Sydney.

The short answer

Choose Yunnan for the best overall balance.

It combines dry winter light, old towns, lakes and genuine snow-mountain scenery without the damp cold of Shanghai or Chongqing. Choose Taiwan instead if convenience, food and rail travel matter most. Choose New Zealand for the strongest road trip and hiking, accepting peak-season prices.

Why choose it

  • The most distinctive single landscape in the comparison.
  • Winter means fewer visitors and the possibility of snow-coated pillars.
  • Furong and Fenghuang add strong culture when mountain visibility is poor.

Plan around

  • Ice and high winds may close cableways, glass paths or roads.
  • Mid-to-late December is preferable to colder early January.

Research notes & sources

Before you book

China entry timing: Australia’s Smartraveller says the current visa waiver for ordinary Australian passport holders is effective through 31 December 2026 and permits eligible stays up to 30 days. If your first entry is in January 2027, do not assume it continues—check again. Smartraveller currently advises Australians to exercise a high degree of caution in China, while Taiwan is listed at normal safety precautions.
